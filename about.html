About STN

Developing the next generation of integrated mental-health infrastructure.

Connecting clinical intelligence, digital innovation, whole-person assessment, therapeutic intervention and coordinated recovery.

Who we are

Supportive Therapy Network Limited.

Supportive Therapy Network Limited (STN) is an emerging New Zealand mental-health and wellbeing organisation developing an integrated model of clinical assessment, digital healthcare innovation, therapeutic intervention, rehabilitation and coordinated whole-person support.

We are building an integrated digital mental-health infrastructure platform designed to transform how individuals are assessed, clinically understood, supported, connected with appropriate services and monitored throughout their mental-health and recovery journeys.

Our work rests on one principle: mental-health care should address the whole person, not only the presenting condition.

Our vision

A more comprehensive, connected and responsive approach to mental-health care.

We aim to bring the following together within one unified support ecosystem.

01

Whole-person psychological assessment

Understanding psychological, emotional, physical, social and practical circumstances together.

02

Structured safety screening

Consistent screening for safety concerns, with human review and escalation.

03

Adaptive assessment pathways

Further assessment focused on the areas that matter for each person.

04

Clinical decision support

Organised, transparent information that supports clinical judgement and never replaces it.

05

Care coordination

Connected referrals and shared plans, so continuity is not lost between services.

06

Therapeutic services

Psychological support and therapeutic planning matched to need.

07

Rehabilitation

Support for recovery, functioning and participation in everyday life.

08

Longitudinal outcome monitoring

Tracking progress over time so support can adapt as needs change.

The challenge

Fragmented systems leave people navigating alone.

In conventional mental-health delivery, individuals frequently encounter fragmented systems in which assessment, diagnosis, treatment, social support, rehabilitation and ongoing care operate independently of one another. Information becomes disconnected, referrals lose continuity, and people are left navigating complex service environments without a clear understanding of where to turn next.

Distress rarely arrives alone. It often intersects with:

  • Addiction
  • Trauma
  • Physical health
  • Housing insecurity
  • Financial hardship
  • Unemployment
  • Social isolation
  • Family circumstances

Yet these needs are typically addressed through separate, disconnected services.

Our response

The person at the centre of a connected pathway.

STN aims to bridge these gaps through a coordinated model that places the individual at the centre of an interconnected pathway of assessment, intervention, support and recovery.

  1. Assessmentleads to understanding.
  2. Understandinginforms intervention.
  3. Interventionis supported by coordinated care and practical assistance.
  4. Recoveryis followed through measurable outcomes.
Our founding principle

More than symptoms. More than a label.

Meaningful mental-health care must extend beyond identifying symptoms or assigning a diagnostic classification. It must consider the wider psychological, emotional, behavioural, physical, social, environmental and functional circumstances influencing an individual's wellbeing.

Technology should strengthen professional practice, not replace it. Diagnostic decisions, clinical formulation, risk management and treatment recommendations remain subject to appropriate professional judgement, clinical governance and applicable standards.

One person. One connected pathway. A more comprehensive approach to care.
