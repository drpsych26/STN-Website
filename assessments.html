Assessments

Two assessments, one intake.

The STN digital assessment platform is designed to run the complete pathway in one application. The Whole Person Initial Assessment feeds directly into the Diagnostic Assessment, with clinician review before any diagnosis is recorded.

Development status. The STN digital assessment platform is in development. It is not yet offered to clients and is not approved for use in care. This page describes the intended design, not a service available today.
The pathway

Ten steps, from consent to follow-up.

  1. Consent and client contextClear information, consent and the person’s background.
  2. Whole Person Initial AssessmentA structured whole-person intake.
  3. Safety GateSafety responses reviewed by a clinician before anything else.
  4. Adaptive screeningFurther screening only where the intake points.
  5. Integrated resultsOne combined picture of needs and strengths.
  6. Diagnostic AssessmentClinician-led structured diagnostic review.
  7. Clinician reviewFindings checked and signed off by a clinician.
  8. Formulation and care planA whole-person plan matched to stepped care.
  9. Finalise and reportA clear summary of findings and next steps.
  10. Follow-up reviewChange tracked over time.
Assessment 1

Whole Person Initial Assessment.

A structured intake covering the whole person. Each area is rated on a five-point scale, from “Not at all” to “Extreme / nearly always”, so that further assessment can focus where it matters.

Mood and energy

Low mood, loss of interest or pleasure, fatigue, sleep, and periods of unusually high energy.

Anxiety, stress and trauma

Worry, panic, trauma-related symptoms, intrusive thoughts and repeated rituals.

Attention and neurodevelopment

Attention and organisation, impulsivity, and social-communication or sensory differences.

Perception and experience

Unusual perceptions or beliefs, and feeling detached or losing time.

Substance use and eating

Alcohol, drugs or non-prescribed medication, and eating, weight or body image.

Safety

Thoughts of self-harm or suicide, risk to others, and feeling unsafe because of violence, abuse or coercive control.

Daily life and circumstances

Anger or loss of control, basic needs such as housing, food, power and transport, and the impact on work, study, parenting, relationships and self-care.

Safety Gate

Safety comes first, and it stays with a person.

Safety-related responses are flagged as the assessment is completed. Each flag must be reviewed and acknowledged by a named clinician before the assessment can move on to review. The software does not make risk decisions on its own.

If you or someone else is in danger now, call 111. For free support at any time, call or text 1737. More options are on the Safety page.

Adaptive screening

Focused where it matters.

Rather than asking every question of everyone, further screening follows the areas the initial assessment highlights. The results are brought together in one integrated summary, giving the clinician a clear, whole-person picture before the Diagnostic Assessment.

Assessment 2

Diagnostic Assessment.

A clinician-led, structured diagnostic review of the areas identified, such as depressive, anxiety, trauma-related and attention-related presentations. The platform organises information against recognised diagnostic criteria to support the clinician. It does not make a diagnosis.

Diagnostic decisions, formulation and treatment recommendations remain the responsibility of the clinician, under clinical governance.

Clinician review and sign-off

Accountable by design.

The platform is designed so that:

  • A clinician reviews every assessment before its results inform care.
  • No one can review or sign off their own work.
  • Signed-off records cannot be altered; corrections create a new version.
  • Every access and change is recorded in a tamper-evident audit trail.
  • Records are encrypted, and clinician sign-in requires multi-factor authentication.
Care planning and follow-up

From results to a plan, and back again.

Results inform a whole-person care plan matched to STN’s stepped-care model. A follow-up assessment then tracks change over time, so the plan can be reviewed and adapted.

Digital tools support structured information gathering and clinical workflow. They do not replace professional judgement or independently make a formal diagnosis. Any third-party questionnaire or rating scale will be used only with the permissions its licence requires.
