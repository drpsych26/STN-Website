Our commitments

Built around safety and accountability.

  • Informed participation and clear information about data use.
  • Human review of assessment findings and clinical decisions.
  • Appropriate escalation when immediate safety concerns arise.
  • Access controls, secure systems and accountable records.
  • Respect for each person’s dignity, rights and preferences.
Safety in the assessment

A Safety Gate in every assessment.

In the STN assessment design, safety-related responses are flagged straight away and must be acknowledged by a named clinician before the assessment can continue. Escalation stays with people, not software. See the assessment pathway.

Privacy

Your information, handled with care.

This website has no forms and does not collect health information. Please do not send health information, crisis details or identity documents by email or phone message.

The STN platform is being designed to handle personal and health information in line with the Privacy Act 2020 and the Health Information Privacy Code 2020. Read the draft privacy notice.

Concerns and complaints

Your rights.

If you have a concern about a health or disability service, you can raise it with the provider, or contact the Health and Disability Commissioner, whose free advocacy service can help you raise it. Visit hdc.org.nz.
