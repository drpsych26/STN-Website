Before you get in touch

Enquiries only, please.

STN services are not yet accepting referrals. Please do not send health information, crisis details or identity documents by phone message or email.

If you are in immediate danger or need urgent mental-health support, call 111 or free call or text 1737, rather than relying on this website. More options are on the Safety page.
