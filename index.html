About STN

Connecting people, services and recovery.

Mental-health needs rarely sit in one place. Assessment, diagnosis, treatment, social support and rehabilitation are often delivered by separate services, and information and referrals can be lost along the way. STN is developing a model that keeps the person at the centre of one connected pathway: from assessment, to clinical understanding, to safety, intervention, rehabilitation, practical support, recovery and outcome monitoring.

STN is an emerging New Zealand organisation. It is developing its digital assessment and clinical decision-support infrastructure alongside therapeutic, rehabilitation and community-navigation services, and it works with partners across health and social support. Technology supports professional practice; it does not replace it.

Founder and Owner/Operator: Dylan Hendriks, Supportive Therapy Network Limited.

The STN approach

More than a diagnosis. A clearer pathway forward.

People’s needs rarely fit into one category. STN’s whole-person model considers mental health, physical wellbeing, relationships, environment, culture, safety and practical circumstances together.

  • Listen to the person’s priorities and lived experience.
  • Understand strengths, needs, risks and protective factors.
  • Coordinate appropriate assessment and therapeutic input.
  • Connect care planning with practical and community supports.
  • Review progress and adapt the pathway as needs change.

Digital assessment

A connected assessment journey.

The STN digital platform is designed to support a consistent pathway from initial intake through clinician review and coordinated care planning.

STAGE 01 · IN DEVELOPMENTWhole-person initial assessment
STAGE 02Clinician-led follow-up and formulation
STAGE 03Clinical review and care planning
STAGE 04Follow-up and outcome review
Development status. The STN digital assessment is in development. It is not yet offered to clients, has not been clinically validated, and is not cleared for use in care. The four stages describe the intended pathway, not a service available today.

Digital tools support structured information gathering and clinical workflow. They do not replace professional judgment or independently make a formal diagnosis. Any service or assessment is subject to appropriate practitioner scope, consent, safety procedures and service availability.

Trust and accountability

Safety, privacy and human oversight matter.

STN’s intended operating model is built around responsible handling of sensitive information, transparent clinical decision support and clear accountability.

  • Informed participation and clear information about data use.
  • Human review of assessment findings and clinical decisions.
  • Appropriate escalation when immediate safety concerns arise.
  • Access controls, secure systems and accountable records.
  • Respect for each person’s dignity, rights and preferences.
