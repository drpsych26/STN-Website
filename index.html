Why STN

Mental-health care is too often fragmented.

People are frequently left to navigate separate systems for assessment, diagnosis, treatment, social support, rehabilitation and ongoing care. Information becomes disconnected, referrals lose continuity, and people are unsure where to turn next.

  • Assessment that leads to understanding.
  • Understanding that informs intervention.
  • Intervention supported by coordinated care and practical assistance.
  • Recovery measured through meaningful outcomes over time.
