Support STN

Help build connected mental-health support.

Contributions help Supportive Therapy Network Limited develop integrated, whole-person mental-health infrastructure for Aotearoa New Zealand.

What your support helps build

From one assessment to a connected pathway.

  • The STN digital assessment platform and its safety design.
  • Clinical governance, privacy and security work needed before services open.
  • Care-coordination and practical-support pathways.
  • Follow-up and outcome monitoring, so recovery can be measured.
