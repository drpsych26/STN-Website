Partnerships

Build connected support with STN.

Supportive Therapy Network Limited welcomes conversations with organisations, practitioners, community groups, agencies and potential collaborators who share an interest in improving whole-person mental-health support in Aotearoa New Zealand.

Working together

Partnerships that strengthen the whole pathway.

STN is developing an integrated model connecting assessment, therapeutic support, rehabilitation and practical care coordination. Partnerships can help build responsible pathways between people and the support they need.

  • Clinical and professional collaboration.
  • Community, iwi and social-service partnerships.
  • Referral pathway development and service coordination.
  • Technology, research and innovation collaboration.
  • Sponsorship, grants and organisational funding discussions.

Current status

Development-stage organisation.

STN services and the STN digital assessment platform remain in development. Services are not yet accepting referrals and are not approved for clinical use. A partnership discussion does not represent a service referral, clinical endorsement, investment offer or commitment by either party.

Please do not send health information, client records or identity documents by email. For urgent mental-health support, call 111 if someone is in immediate danger or contact 1737.
