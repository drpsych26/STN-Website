Services

Connected support, built around the person.

The services below are planned and in development. They describe what STN is building, not services available today.

Development status. STN services are not yet accepting referrals and are not offered through this website. Service availability, referral eligibility and the regions covered will be published here once confirmed.
Planned services

Six connected services, one pathway.

01 / ASSESSMENT

Whole-person assessment

Structured exploration of presenting concerns, functioning, history, strengths, safety and support needs.

02 / DIAGNOSTIC

Clinician-led diagnostic assessment

Structured diagnostic review of the areas identified, completed with a clinician and supported by organised information.

03 / THERAPEUTIC

Mental health and wellbeing support

Pathways for psychological support, therapeutic planning and appropriate referral.

04 / REHABILITATION

Rehabilitation and recovery

Support for recovery, functioning and returning to the activities and roles that matter.

05 / COORDINATION

Practical care navigation

Support planning that recognises housing, essentials, advocacy and community connection as part of wellbeing.

06 / FOLLOW-UP

Follow-up and outcome review

Follow-up assessment and review, so progress is measured and the plan can adapt.

Stepped care

The right level of support at the right time.

STN’s planned stepped-care model matches the level of support to each person’s needs, and steps up or down as those needs change.

  1. Self-management and community supportInformation, self-help resources and connection to community supports.
  2. Guided self-help and brief interventionShort, structured support for mild or emerging difficulties.
  3. Structured psychological therapyPlanned therapy for needs that call for more than brief support.
  4. Specialist and complex care, with case managementCoordinated specialist input and case management for complex or co-occurring needs.
  5. Acute crisis responseImmediate safety needs are directed to emergency and crisis services. STN is not an emergency service.
Practical support

Wellbeing depends on the basics too.

Housing, food, power, transport, finances, work and family circumstances all shape mental health. STN’s care coordination is designed to connect people with practical and community supports alongside clinical care, so that one need is not addressed in isolation from the rest.
