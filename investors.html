Why STN

Connecting what is currently fragmented.

Assessment, diagnosis, treatment, social support, rehabilitation and ongoing care are too often delivered separately. STN is developing one connected pathway, supported by a digital assessment platform built with human oversight, privacy and security at its core.

  • Whole-person assessment and adaptive pathways.
  • Clinician review and accountable sign-off, by design.
  • Care coordination, rehabilitation and outcome monitoring.
  • Built for New Zealand privacy and health-information requirements.
