Legal, licensing & clinical governance

This page describes the legal, intellectual-property, privacy and clinical-governance framework relevant to Supportive Therapy Network Limited (STN), its website and its digital assessment platform.

Register status: This is STN's public information framework. Specific certificates, registration numbers, licence references, audit dates and expiry dates will only be published when verified against the underlying documents and approved for public disclosure. A statement on this page is not a substitute for a certificate or regulator's decision.

1. Organisation and intellectual property

© 2026 Supportive Therapy Network Limited. All rights reserved.

Unless a specific third-party notice states otherwise, STN's proprietary materials include its source code, software architecture, documentation, original designs, assessment workflows, branding and other materials created and owned by STN.

No permission is granted to use, copy, modify, distribute, sublicense, publish, deploy or commercially exploit STN-owned materials without prior written permission from Supportive Therapy Network Limited.

This notice does not override third-party copyright, open-source licences, assessment publisher terms, font licences or other third-party rights. Those terms continue to apply independently.

2. Clinical certification and accreditation register

STN maintains an internal evidence register for applicable provider certifications, audits, accreditations, approvals and professional credentials. Public entries should identify the issuing authority, certificate or registration reference, scope, legal entity or practitioner covered, issue date, expiry or review date, and verification source.

Public certificate schedule: No certificate number or accreditation claim is published in this version because the underlying certificate records have not yet been supplied for verification and publication approval.

A professional's registration or practising certificate is not the same thing as certification of an organisation, facility or service.

3. Professional practice and clinical accountability

Clinical services must be delivered only by appropriately qualified, registered and authorised practitioners acting within their lawful scope of practice and current practising requirements. The responsible practitioner retains accountability for clinical assessment, formulation, diagnosis, treatment and referral decisions.

STN's digital tools are intended to support structured information gathering and clinical workflows. They do not replace practitioner judgement, informed consent, appropriate supervision or independent clinical decision-making.

4. Assessment instruments and third-party materials

Each proprietary assessment instrument, questionnaire, scale, scoring algorithm, clinical framework, font, software package and other third-party component must be used only where the applicable permission or licence allows the specific use, reproduction, scoring, electronic implementation, commercial use and distribution.

STN maintains an instrument and dependency register recording the rights holder, version, permitted use, restrictions, evidence of permission and renewal or review date. The presence of an instrument in a prototype does not itself establish permission to use it in a live service.

5. Privacy and health information

Privacy notice status: draft. A reviewed and approved privacy notice, including the privacy officer contact, will be published before this website collects any personal information.

STN must handle personal and health information in accordance with applicable New Zealand privacy and health-information requirements, including the Privacy Act 2020 and the Health Information Privacy Code 2020 as amended or replaced from time to time.

People should be given clear information about what information is collected, why it is needed, how it is used and disclosed, how it is protected, and how they can exercise applicable access and correction rights. Consent and lawful authority must be addressed before information is collected or shared where required.

Do not send clinical records, diagnoses, medication details or other sensitive health information through this public website. This website is not an emergency or clinical intake service.

6. Consumer rights, complaints and safety

Where STN provides health or disability services, applicable consumer rights and service obligations must be observed, including informed choice and consent, effective communication, respect, reasonable care and skill, continuity of services where required, and access to a fair complaints process.

Service-specific safety procedures must identify responsibility for urgent escalation, emergency response, after-hours arrangements, referrals and continuity of care. Website enquiries are not monitored as a crisis response channel.

7. Digital platform and intended use

The STN Whole Person Assessment platform is designed to support structured assessment workflows, screening, clinician review, documentation and care planning. Its actual regulatory classification depends on its intended purpose, claims and implemented functionality.

STN will not describe the software as regulator-approved, certified as a medical device, clinically validated for a particular purpose or authorised for production use unless the relevant evidence and applicable regulatory determination support that exact claim.

8. Security, access and records

Production clinical systems require appropriate authentication, role-based access, secure storage and transmission, audit logging, backup and recovery, retention and disposal controls, incident response, and tested access and correction processes. Access should be limited to authorised people with a legitimate need.

Public code repositories and public website forms must not be used to store client records, identifiable health information, credentials, signing keys or other secrets.

9. Governance documents and version control

STN maintains controlled versions of its policies, clinical procedures, risk and safety documentation, software releases, instrument permissions and certification evidence. Material changes to assessment content, scoring, diagnostic support or safety pathways require documented clinical review and approval before release.

For legal or regulatory enquiries, use STN's published contact channel. Do not submit confidential certificates or client information through public issue trackers.

10. Legal notice and limitations

This page is general information about STN's governance framework. It is not legal advice, a regulator's determination, proof of certification, a professional credential verification service or a guarantee that a particular service or software release is authorised for use.

Applicable requirements depend on the actual service, premises, staffing, intended use, contracts and deployment. The relevant regulator or qualified legal adviser should be consulted where a formal determination is required.
